# Výber peňazí z firmy – postup

Facts: `knowledge/vyber-penazi-z-firmy.md`, rates in `knowledge/sadzby-a-limity.md`.
Statutes: `laws/zdp/current.md` (§ 5, § 6, § 15, § 43), `laws/socialne-poistenie/current.md`,
`laws/zdravotne-poistenie/current.md`, `laws/obchodny-zakonnik/current.md` (rozdelenie zisku s.r.o.).

1. Clarify the goal: one-off amount now, yearly plan, pension/social coverage matters?
2. Options: dividends (from approved profit; withholding 7 % for 2025+ profits, 10 % for 2024 profits), remuneration
   (deductible for company; income tax + contributions), travel allowances, rent, loan repayment.
3. If amounts are given, compute a clear comparison: company tax → dividend tax → net; vs. gross remuneration →
   employer contributions → employee contributions → income tax → net. Show the arithmetic briefly; state which rates
   you used and mark unverified ones.
4. Legal constraints: dividends only after approving financial statements; no distribution causing insolvency;
   hidden profit distribution risks (loans to shareholder, private expenses).
5. For a full plan (salary vs dividend optimum) → deep mode: delegate to `mzdy-odvody-specialista` and
   `dan-z-prijmov-specialista`.

Never present a computed comparison as precise if any input rate is ⚠️; say "orientačne".
