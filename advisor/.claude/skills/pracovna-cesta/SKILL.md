# Pracovná cesta – postup

Facts: `knowledge/pracovne-cesty.md`; statute: `laws/cestovne-nahrady/current.md` (zákon 283/2002 Z. z.).

1. Is it a business trip? (outside the regular workplace, for business purpose, ordered by the employer – the konateľ
   orders it for themselves). Commuting to the regular workplace is not a business trip.
2. Who travels? Employee or konateľ (also without employment contract → covered by 283/2002).
3. Duration → stravné band (5–12 h, 12–18 h, over 18 h). Current amounts are in `knowledge/sadzby-a-limity.md`;
   if the statute or a newer MPSVR announcement exists, prefer it (grep "stravné" / check `laws/CHANGELOG.md`).
4. Free meals provided (paid by the company card, included in hotel/conference)? → reduce stravné
   (verify percentages in § 5 of 283/2002 by grepping "kráti" / "bezplatne").
5. Transport: company car (fuel by logbook) vs. private car (basic km allowance + fuel per TP consumption) vs. public
   transport/taxi (actual cost with receipt).
6. Abroad: foreign stravné per country (MF SR measure), bands, optional vreckové; verify current amounts on the web
   (Finančná správa / MF SR) if asked for a concrete country.
7. Tax: deductible up to statutory entitlement (§ 19 ods. 2 písm. d) ZDP); above → non-deductible; for the
   recipient tax-free within limits.

Common combo: trip to a client + lunch with the client → trip costs deductible, client's lunch = representation (❌).
