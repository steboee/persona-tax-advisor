# Nastavenie s.r.o. – postup

1. Read `knowledge/nastavenie-sro-sluzby.md`, and the user's facts in `profile/company.md` + `profile/memory.md`
   (already in context). Note the phase (founding / first year / running).
2. Identify the decisive facts; if missing, assume the typical case (one person selling services, clients mostly
   businesses, revenue < 100 000 €) and state the assumption: type of services and clients (SR / EU / non-EU),
   expected revenue and costs, other income/insurance of the user, need for regular income (mortgage, sick pay,
   parental benefits), partner's real role and other income.
3. Give a concrete recommendation (not just options): which remuneration model now, when to revisit
   (e.g. revenue > 100 000 €, mortgage, planning kids), VAT steps (§ 7a before the first EU service), first-week checklist.
4. Numbers: if the user gives amounts, compute net results for 2–3 variants (company tax → dividend vs. remuneration
   with contributions). Mark unverified rates ⚠️ and say "orientačne". In deep mode delegate the computation to
   `mzdy-odvody-specialista`.
5. Partner employment: only real work at a fair wage; list evidence to keep; show cost vs. benefit
   (income splitting, her insurance/maternity coverage) and the audit risk.
6. When the user agrees on a decision → save it with the `pamat` skill.
