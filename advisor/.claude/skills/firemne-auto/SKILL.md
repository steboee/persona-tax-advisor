# Firemné auto – postup

Facts: `knowledge/firemne-auto.md`. Statutes: `laws/dph/current.md` (§ 49, § 85n), `laws/zdp/current.md`
(§ 5 ods. 3 písm. a), § 19, § 22–28), `laws/dan-z-motorovych-vozidiel/current.md`.

1. Which car? Passenger M1 / truck N1 (different VAT rules), acquired when (before / after 1. 1. 2026), price incl. VAT,
   purchase / financial lease / operating lease / private car of the konateľ.
2. VAT (if VAT payer): M1 acquired or leased 1. 1. 2026 – 30. 6. 2028 → 50 % deduction unless exclusively business use
   with detailed records + notification (§ 85n). Before 2026 → proportional deduction by business use.
3. Income tax: depreciation (group, limit for expensive cars – grep "48 000" in `laws/zdp/current.md`),
   operating costs, logbook requirement, private-use split.
4. Private use by konateľ/employee → 1 % of entry price monthly as non-cash income (§ 5 ods. 3 písm. a)), payroll
   tax and contributions; fuel for private trips separately.
5. Private car used for the company → travel allowances on business trips (simplest) or rental agreement.
6. Motor vehicle tax (filing by 31. 1.).
7. For buy-vs-lease or "should I buy a car through the company" → deep analysis: compute total after-tax cost,
   delegate to `dph-specialista` + `dan-z-prijmov-specialista` in deep mode.

Mark anything ⚠️ in knowledge as needing verification and grep the statute before stating it as fact.
