# Reálne možnosti – postup

Read `knowledge/realne-moznosti.md` (catalog) and use facts from `profile/company.md` + `profile/memory.md`.

1. Identify what the user really wants: a thing (TV, phone, car, trip) or cash.
2. Ask yourself: can the thing genuinely serve the business (office, client work, travel)? If yes → that's the 🟢/🟡 deductible path with conditions.
3. If it's private: list the legal ways the company card can still pay it:
   pohľadávka → zápočet s dividendou (cost ≈ dividend tax 7 % on top of corporate tax), repay to the company,
   non-cash income (payroll tax + contributions). Compare their real cost in one line each.
4. Add structural tips that fit the situation (shareholder loan instead of equity at founding, home-office rent,
   company car with 1 %, travel allowances, partner employment for real work, mixed-use electronics).
5. If the user suggests booking a private item as a company expense: say clearly it's illegal (🔴), give realistic
   consequences, and immediately offer the closest legal alternative. Don't moralize beyond one sentence.
6. Format: CLAUDE.md answer format; in the details show 2–4 options as a compact list:
   `🟢 Option – what to do – cost/tax impact`. The spoken part names the best legal option.
